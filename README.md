# Coffee Chain Tableau Analysis

## Project Overview

This Tableau project analyses Coffee Chain sales and profit performance across regions, products and time periods.

The project focuses on using Tableau visualisations to answer business questions around regional performance, product profitability, sales trends and forecasting.

## Tools Used

- Tableau
- Data visualisation
- Dashboarding
- Business analysis
- Forecasting

## Business Questions

The analysis answers questions such as:

- Which markets generate the most revenue, profit and order volume?
- Which regions are above or below the company average and sales target?
- Which products drive the most profit?
- Is Coffee Chain showing a growth or decline trajectory?
- What are the expected sales for the next six months?

## Key Insights

- The West region performed strongest across key measures including sales, profit and quantity sold.
- West was the only region above both the company average sales value and the assumed sales target.
- Latte generated the highest profit, while Americano produced the lowest profit among the products shown.
- Sales showed regular month-to-month fluctuations, with a mostly flat trend line and a slight downward slope.
- The six-month forecast suggested sales may remain relatively stable, although the wide confidence band shows uncertainty.
